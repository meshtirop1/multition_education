Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

1. Introduction

MultiTion Education ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our online education platform.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information

When you register, we collect:

Your full name, email address, username, and profile picture (optional). If you register via social authentication (Google, GitHub, or LinkedIn), we receive your name, email, and profile picture from those services.

2.2 Usage Data

We automatically collect information about how you interact with the platform, including: courses enrolled, exercise submissions, progress data, forum posts, messages sent, login timestamps, and browser/device information.

2.3 Cookies

We use essential cookies for authentication and session management. We ask for your consent before setting any non-essential cookies. You can manage your cookie preferences at any time.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to: provide and maintain our educational services, process your enrollment and track course progress, generate certificates upon course completion, facilitate communication between students and mentors, send notifications about your courses and submissions, improve our platform and user experience, and ensure the security of our services.

4. Third-Party Authentication

When you log in using Google, GitHub, or LinkedIn, we only receive the information you authorize those services to share (typically name, email, and profile picture). We do not access your contacts, posts, repositories, or other private data from those services. Each provider's own privacy policy governs how they handle your data.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share your data only with: your assigned course mentors (name, email, progress, submissions), platform administrators (for account approval and management), and other students only through your voluntary forum posts and community interactions. We may disclose information if required by law.

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures including encrypted passwords, secure session handling, and access controls. However, no electronic transmission or storage method is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. Course progress, submissions, and certificates are retained indefinitely to maintain your academic record. You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to: access the personal data we hold about you, correct inaccurate information via your profile settings, request deletion of your account, withdraw consent for cookie tracking, and export your course progress and certificate data.

9. Children's Privacy

Our platform is not intended for children under 16. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 16. If we become aware of such collection, we will delete the information promptly.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify registered users of significant changes via email or platform notification.

11. Contact Us

For privacy-related questions or requests, contact us at: privacy@multition.edu
